## Executive Summary

Sky Blue 11 Company Limited (HKEX: 1010.HK) stands at an operational and balance sheet inflection point as of **September 28, 2026**. Incorporated in Bermuda and headquartered in Wan Chai, Hong Kong, the company operates as an investment holding group with a micro-cap equity valuation ranging between HK$24 million and HK$27 million (based on 336.59 million issued ordinary shares). Formerly known as **Balk 1798 Group Limited** and **PacRay International Holdings Limited**, the firm underwent a formal corporate rebranding to Sky Blue 11 Company Limited in February 2024.

The group’s active operations span four distinct segments:
1. **Integrated Circuits (IC) & Semiconductor Distribution (Core Engine):** Sourcing, agency, and distribution of IC components used in industrial and household electronic devices across Mainland China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan.
2. **Executive Jet / Aviation Services:** Aircraft management, charter brokerage, and pilot training.
3. **Yacht Manufacturing & Sales:** Custom luxury marine manufacturing, maintenance, and asset management.
4. **Property Investment:** Commercial real estate holdings generating rental income.

In December 2025, the board altered the financial year-end from December 31 to June 30, creating an extended **18-month reporting period** spanning January 1, 2025, to June 30, 2026. On September 23, 2026, management issued an inside information profit warning projecting a narrowed net loss of **HK$65 million to HK$70 million** for this 18-month cycle—a major improvement over the HK$165.1 million net loss recorded in FY2024. This reduction was primarily driven by a collapse in financial and non-financial asset impairments, which fell from over HK$129 million in FY2024 to under HK$4 million.

The scheduled **Board Meeting on September 28, 2026**, serves as the critical operational catalyst. The board will review and approve the 18-month audited financial results, address ongoing going-concern considerations, and deliberate on the potential recommendation of a final dividend.

## Forensic Accounting Audit

### Governing Framework & Standards Application

The financial examination evaluates Sky Blue 11 under **Hong Kong Financial Reporting Standards (HKFRS)**, aligned with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS):

* **HKAS 32 / HKFRS 9 (Financial Instruments):** Requires debt vs. equity disaggregation for hybrid capital, preference shares, or convertible debt issued during corporate restructurings. Mandatory cash redemption terms require liability classification, with coupon payments charged through profit and loss (P&L) as finance costs.
* **HKAS 23 (Borrowing Costs):** Interest costs tied to qualifying capital assets (yacht manufacturing yards, property developments) can only be capitalized during active development. Once assets are available for use or development is paused, interest must be expensed directly through P&L.
* **HKAS 16 / HKAS 36 (Property, Plant & Equipment & Asset Impairment):** HKAS 16 mandates depreciation onset as soon as assets are ready for intended use. The drop in non-cash asset impairments from >HK$129 million (FY2024) to <HK$4 million (18-month cycle) requires verification to confirm prior write-downs were not excessively front-loaded ("Big Bath" accounting) to artificially depress current depreciation expense.
* **HKAS 21 (Foreign Currency Exchange):** Cross-border IC distribution in RMB, TWD, and HKD requires functional currency translation. RMB assets and liabilities are translated at period-end spot rates, while income statement items use weighted-average rates. Accumulated translation reserves are maintained in Other Comprehensive Income (OCI).

1. **Working Capital & Trade Credit Deficit:** Micro-cap credit constraints restrict trade financing. While upstream semiconductor component procurement demands prompt payment or strict 30-day supplier credit terms, downstream electronics buyers require 60-to-90-day payment terms. Working capital availability serves as the primary operational constraint on unit volume.
2. **Cross-Border Customs & Regulatory Inspection:** Component movement across Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and Taiwan is subject to regulatory origin checks, dual-use technology screening, and customs clearances. Inspection holds introduce supply chain friction, increasing required buffer stock and lowering annual inventory turn velocity.
3. **Upstream Component Allocation Tiering:** Top-tier semiconductor foundries prioritize high-volume global distributors. As a micro-cap distributor, Sky Blue 11 operates in a lower allocation tier, exposing its inventory intake to industry-wide supply fluctuations.
4. **Legacy Overhead Drag:** Operational costs associated with yacht shipyards and executive aviation leases (insurance, maintenance, storage) represent ongoing overhead that reduces cash available for semiconductor inventory procurement.

### Strategic Supply Chain Roadmap

* **Transition to Bonded Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI):** Shift inventory holding entirely into 3PL bonded logistics hubs within the Shenzhen Free Trade Zone to minimize fixed facility overhead and lower customs clearance lead times.
* **Accounts Receivable Factoring:** Execute non-recourse receivables factoring to shorten the effective cash conversion cycle, freeing working capital and raising the core segment's throughput capacity without requiring equity dilution.
* **Legacy Asset Realization:** Complete a formal operational exit from yacht shipyard facilities, subleasing or liquidating real estate assets to reduce overhead drag and reallocate capital toward core IC distribution operations.
