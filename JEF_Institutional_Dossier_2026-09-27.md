## Forensic Accounting Audit

### Governing Framework & Accounting Standards Mapping (US GAAP)

#### Debt Classification & Interest Accrual (ASC 470 & ASC 835-30)
The $1.5 billion 5.500% Senior Notes (due 2036) and €850 million 4.500% Senior Notes (due 2033) represent senior uncollateralized obligations under ASC 470. Issuance costs are capitalized as direct debt deductions and amortized using the effective interest method under ASC 835-30. 
* **P&L Impact:** Annual coupon obligations equal $82.50 million on the USD notes and €38.25 million on the EUR notes. Net quarterly corporate interest expense is recognized directly in the Consolidated Statement of Earnings at approximately **$31.25 million** across both tranches.

#### SMBC Governance Alignment & Equity Method Investment (ASC 323 / ASC 321 / ASC 825)
The nomination of SMBC executive Yoshihiro Hyakutome to Jefferies' Board of Directors establishes significant influence under ASC 323, requiring Equity Method accounting for shared governance entities unless the fair value option under ASC 825 is elected.

#### Assets Held for Sale & Impairment Rules (ASC 360-10 & ASC 205-20)
The binding agreement to divest Tessellis mandates reclassification to Assets Held for Sale.
* **Accounting Mandate:** Carrying value must be recorded at the **lower of carrying amount or fair value less cost to sell**. Asset depreciation and equity-method income pickups cease immediately upon classification. Any valuation shortfall requires an immediate non-cash impairment write-down in current period earnings.

#### Foreign Currency Remeasurement & Hedging Dynamics (ASC 830 & ASC 815)
The €850 million Senior Notes create direct foreign currency liability exposure on the USD-denominated balance sheet.
* **P&L Risk Exposure:** Unless formally designated as a Net Investment Hedge (NIH) against European operating subsidiaries under ASC 815, foreign currency fluctuations must be remeasured into USD at each balance sheet date through net income under ASC 830. A **1.0% appreciation in the EUR/USD exchange rate generates ~$9.25 million in unrealized pre-tax FX losses**.
