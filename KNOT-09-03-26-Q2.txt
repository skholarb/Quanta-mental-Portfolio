Deepwater Floating Production Storage and Offloading (FPSO) units lack subsea pipeline infrastructure to shore; without continuous DP shuttle tanker offloading, FPSO storage buffers breach within days, forcing well shut-ins. This dynamic locks in high contract renewal urgency among Tier-1 operators.

* Downstream Off-Take Lock-In: Multi-year commitments—anchored by the newly acquired 2024-built Hedda Knutsen (chartered to Petrobras through November 2034) and the Ingrid Knutsen 3-year extension with Eni—secure an $881.2M firm revenue backlog, immunizing daily operating cash flows against spot shipping volatility.

Downstream Charter Backlog: Contracted Revenue Stability
* Balancing these balance sheet obligations is an $881.2M firm contracted backlog with high-credit counterparties (100% contracted for 2H 2026; ~97% for 1H 2027).
* Downstream charterers capitalize these commitments under IFRS 16 / ASC 842 as long-term lease liabilities, ensuring KNOP’s cash flow velocity remains insulated from broader maritime commodity spot rate swings.
