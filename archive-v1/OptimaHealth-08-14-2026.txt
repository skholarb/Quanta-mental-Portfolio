Subject: Optima Health PLC (LON: OPT) — Fundamental Equity Update & Scenario Modeling
Ticker: OPT.L (AIM)
Executive Summary
Optima Health PLC is scaling rapidly as the UK’s leading technology-enabled corporate health and wellbeing provider. Following the transformational £100 million acquisition of PAM Healthcare Limited in March 2026, the business has re-engineered its operational footprint.
With FY26 revenue printing at ~£121 million (a 15% year-on-year increase) and adjusted EBITDA tracking c.10% ahead of market consensus (£18.1 million baseline), Optima is executing on its structural roadmap toward medium-term targets of £200 million revenue and £40 million adjusted EBITDA.
Key Fundamental Drivers
The operational architecture relies on structural scaling, strict internal controls, and network consolidation. Based on public filings and operational disclosures, the fundamental drivers are categorized below:
1. External Scale & Network Consolidation (The "Frictionless Ecosystem")
* PAM Healthcare Integration Velocity: The £100 million acquisition of PAM significantly expands B2B and public sector reach. Initial integration has moved rapidly, with £1.3 million in annualized cost synergies already realized as of June 2026.
* Public Sector and Legal Windfalls: The resolution of the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) procurement dispute yielded £4.7 million in total other operating income (£2.3 million in H1, £2.4 million in H2), solidifying institutional credibility and cash generation.
* Contract Pipeline Realization: Core annualized new business wins for Optima standalone stood at £10.8 million in FY26, underpinned by major frameworks such as the UK Armed Forces medical assessment contract delivered alongside Serco.
2. Internal Margin Protection & Anti-Chaos Infrastructure (The "Internal Support Framework")
* Operating Leverage & Cost Disciplines: Managing a combined workforce of over 2,000 staff requires rigorous operational controls. Optima's digital triage platform reduces administrative friction, protecting EBITDA margins against UK wage inflation and National Insurance headwinds.
* Balance Sheet Management: Following the acquisition debt load, the group completed an underwritten Open Offer equity raise in April 2026, successfully paying down the £30 million shareholder bridging loan and stabilizing net debt (excluding leases) at £94.4 million at the fiscal year-end, with leverage trending down as EBITDA expands.

Investment Conclusion & Valuation Outlook
Optima Health occupies an entrenched market position with high barriers to entry due to its proprietary technology platform and accredited clinical infrastructure.
* The Base Case View: The successful absorption of PAM Healthcare and realization of early cost synergies validate management's capital allocation strategy. As the business amortizes integration costs and scales digital delivery, conversion of top-line expansion (£121M) into free cash flow will justify a re-rating on the AIM market.
* Risk Factors: Key risks include wage inflation in clinical staff, delayed integration milestones for acquired entities, and potential execution risks on large-scale public sector tenders.
