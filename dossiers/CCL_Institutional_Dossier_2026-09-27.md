## Forensic Accounting Audit

### 1. Regulatory Accounting Standards Alignment
As a Dual-Listed Company, Carnival reports consolidated balance sheets under U.S. GAAP (Form 10-K/10-Q) while maintaining statutory filings for Carnival plc under IFRS and UK GAAP.

```
       Dual-Listed Enterprise Structure (Carnival Corp & plc)
                       |
        +--------------+--------------+
        |                             |
Carnival Corporation            Carnival plc
(Panama / NYSE)                (UK / LSE & NYSE)
   [U.S. GAAP]                    [IFRS / UK GAAP]
        |                             |
        +--------------+--------------+
                       |
         Consolidated Financial Engine
```

* **Convertible Debt Retirement (ASC 470-20 / ASU 2020-06):** Under ASU 2020-06, convertible debt is evaluated as a single liability unit. Settling this debt in cash triggers a derecognition of the debt liability on the balance sheet, with any difference between cash paid and net carrying value recognized as an early extinguishment item. Cash settlement prevents dilution, removing 18 million shares from diluted EPS calculations under ASC 260.
* **Interest Capitalization Boundaries (ASC 835-20):** Interest expense is capitalized for vessels under construction at shipyards. Capitalization ceases immediately on the date the vessel is delivered and placed into service. Post-delivery interest converts directly into a recurring income statement expense, requiring new vessel revenue to offset the added interest costs.
* **Property, Plant, and Equipment Depreciation (ASC 360):** Cruise ships are depreciated on a straight-line basis over an estimated 30-year useful life, assuming a 15% residual value. Land-side assets (e.g., Celebration Key) begin depreciation when open for operations. Dry-docking expenses are capitalized and amortized over 30 to 60 months.
* **Foreign Currency Mechanics (ASC 830):** Operating units use local functional currencies (USD for Carnival Cruise Line and Princess; EUR for Costa and AIDA; GBP for P&O UK and Cunard). Non-functional currency transactions are remeasured into the functional currency through net income, while statement translation adjustments flow through Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income (AOCI).

Delays can force higher transit speeds to make the next port call, increasing fuel burn.
* **Node 2: Maritime Geopolitical Constraints:** Geopolitical instability near the Suez Canal forces vessels to navigate around the Cape of Good Hope. This adds 10 to 11 transit days between Europe and Asia/Indian Ocean destinations, consuming operational itinerary days in non-revenue transit while increasing fuel burn per voyage by 25% to 35%.

```
[Normal Route]   Med / Europe ◄──────── Suez Canal ────────► Indian Ocean (11-14 Days)
```

* **Node 5: Shipyard Dry-Dock Availability:** Mega-cruise vessels (>150,000 gross tons) rely on a limited number of global shipyards with dry-docks large enough to service them.
